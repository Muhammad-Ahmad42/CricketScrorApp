Scorebook Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Scorebook (“Cricket Scorer – Scorebook”) is an offline cricket scoring and tournament app published by Ahmad Khan. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Summary

  • Scorebook does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising in the app.
  • The app does not use the internet. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information you enter

Teams, player names, matches, scores and tournaments that you enter are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. We cannot see or access them.

Backups, PDFs and sharing

When you choose to export a backup file or share a PDF scorecard, the app creates the file on your device and hands it to the destination you pick (for example a messaging app, email or file storage). What happens to the file after that is governed by the app or service you chose.

Permissions

Scorebook only uses vibration, for optional haptic feedback while scoring. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Deleting your data

You can delete matches, tournaments and teams inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Scorebook does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Google Play

Google Play may collect information about app installs and crashes under Google’s own privacy policy. Scorebook does not add any data collection of its own.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data differently (for example advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the updated date will be shown above.
